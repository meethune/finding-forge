## Description

Missing rate limits or quotas enables DoS and brute force. CWE-770 ranked #25.

## Impact

Service outage, credential stuffing success, and cloud cost exhaustion.

## Mitigation

Rate limiting; CAPTCHA where appropriate; account lockout policies; WAF; resource quotas.

## Replication Steps

1. Send high-volume requests to login, search, or expensive APIs.
2. Measure throttling and lockout.
3. Attempt password spraying.
4. Document absence of limits.
5. Assess business impact.

**Network:** Traffic spikes to auth endpoints; repeated 401/403 from single sources.
