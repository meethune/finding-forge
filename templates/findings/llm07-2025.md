## Description

System prompts or hidden instructions may be extracted, reducing effectiveness of safety controls and revealing business logic or secrets embedded in prompts.

## Impact

Exposure aids further attacks (prompt injection, tool abuse) and may leak confidential instructions or credentials in prompts.

## Mitigation

Avoid secrets in system prompts; separate instructions from user-visible context; monitor extraction attempts; rotate exposed instructions.

## Replication Steps

1. Use known prompt extraction techniques on the interface.
2. Compare leaked text to suspected system instructions.
3. Assess whether leakage reveals tools, policies, or credentials.
4. Document reproducible extraction.
5. Rate severity by sensitivity of leaked content.
