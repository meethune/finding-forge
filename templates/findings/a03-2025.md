## Description

Supply chain failures are breakdowns in building, distributing, or updating software—often via vulnerable, outdated, or malicious third-party components, tools, or dependencies. Scope includes transitive dependencies, CI/CD compromise, and untrusted package sources.

## Impact

Compromise can affect all consumers of a component (RCE, data theft, credential harvesting on developer machines). Incidents such as SolarWinds, npm worms, and wallet supply-chain attacks demonstrate systemic blast radius.

## Mitigation

Maintain SBOM; track direct and transitive dependencies; monitor CVE/NVD/OSV; use signed artifacts; harden CI/CD with separation of duties and MFA; obtain components from trusted registries; staged rollouts; virtual patch when upgrade is blocked.

## Replication Steps

1. Inventory application and build dependencies (package manifests, container images).
2. Compare versions against known CVE databases.
3. Review CI/CD pipeline permissions and secret handling.
4. Verify package signatures and provenance where available.
5. Document outdated or unmaintained components.
