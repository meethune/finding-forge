## Description

Concurrent operations on shared resources without proper synchronization. Mapped under A06:2025.

## Impact

Double spending, duplicate rewards, or TOCTOU bypass of security checks.

## Mitigation

Transactions; locking; idempotency keys; serialize critical sections.

## Replication Steps

1. Identify high-value concurrent workflows.
2. Send parallel requests with scripting.
3. Observe inconsistent state.
4. Document timing window.
5. Recommend synchronization.
