## Description

The application does not verify that state-changing requests were intentionally submitted by the authenticated user. CWE-352 ranked #3 in CWE Top 25:2025.

## Impact

Forced actions on behalf of victims (password change, transfers, privilege changes) when combined with session cookies.

## Mitigation

Anti-CSRF tokens tied to session; SameSite cookies; re-authentication for sensitive actions; verify Origin/Referer.

## Replication Steps

1. Capture a state-changing request as an authenticated user.
2. Replay from another origin/browser without token.
3. Confirm action succeeds.
4. Test token validation and SameSite behavior.
5. Document affected functions.
