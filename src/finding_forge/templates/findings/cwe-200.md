## Description

Sensitive data is revealed to unauthorized parties. CWE-200 ranked #20.

## Impact

Disclosure of PII, credentials, internal paths, or configuration aids further attacks.

## Mitigation

Minimize data returned; access controls; error handling; classify data; encrypt sensitive fields.

## Replication Steps

1. Review responses, errors, and metadata for sensitive fields.
2. Access resources without proper authorization.
3. Document disclosed data types.
4. Assess regulatory impact.
5. Recommend redaction.
