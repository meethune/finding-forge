## Description

LLM supply chain risks include compromised models, plugins, training datasets, hosting platforms, and third-party APIs without integrity verification.

## Impact

Malicious models or plugins can exfiltrate data, provide backdoors, or subvert safety alignment across deployments.

## Mitigation

Vet model and plugin sources; pin versions; SBOM for AI stack; scan artifacts; monitor vendor advisories; sign and verify packages.

## Replication Steps

1. Inventory models, embeddings, plugins, and inference endpoints.
2. Review provenance and update mechanisms.
3. Check for known vulnerable components in AI pipeline.
4. Assess plugin permission scopes.
5. Document untrusted or unverifiable components.
