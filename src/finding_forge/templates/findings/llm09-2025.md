## Description

LLMs may produce false, outdated, or biased content presented as authoritative, affecting decisions in high-stakes domains.

## Impact

Financial, medical, legal, or safety decisions based on hallucinations can cause harm and liability.

## Mitigation

Ground responses with verified sources; confidence calibration; human review for critical decisions; disclaimers; retrieval from authoritative corpora.

## Replication Steps

1. Query model on verifiable facts in scope.
2. Measure hallucination rate on test set.
3. Assess whether UI presents outputs as guaranteed truth.
4. Review safeguards for regulated advice.
5. Document harmful incorrect outputs.
