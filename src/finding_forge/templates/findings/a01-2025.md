## Description

Access control enforces policy so users cannot act outside intended permissions. Failures allow unauthorized disclosure, modification, or destruction of data, or execution of business functions beyond authorized scope. Common issues include missing deny-by-default, IDOR, forced browsing, privilege escalation, CORS misconfiguration, and SSRF.

## Impact

Attackers may access other users' accounts or data, perform administrative actions, or pivot through internal services. Impact spans confidentiality, integrity, and availability depending on exposed functions.

## Mitigation

Enforce access control in trusted server-side code; deny by default except public resources; reuse centralized authorization; enforce record ownership; disable directory listing; log and alert on access control failures; rate-limit APIs; invalidate sessions on logout; validate JWT audience and expiry.

## Replication Steps

1. Authenticate as a low-privilege user.
2. Identify object references (IDs, UUIDs) in requests.
3. Modify identifiers to target another user's resources.
4. Attempt privileged endpoints (e.g., /admin) without appropriate role.
5. Document unauthorized access with request/response evidence.

## Detection

**Network:** Monitor for 403/401 spikes, abnormal access to admin paths, and SSRF patterns to internal RFC1918 targets.
