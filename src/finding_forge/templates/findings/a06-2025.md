## Description

Insecure design reflects missing or ineffective security controls at the architecture and requirements level—distinct from implementation bugs. Includes flawed business logic, weak threat modeling, and absence of secure-by-design practices.

## Impact

Design flaws may enable fraud, abuse of workflows, credential recovery bypass, race conditions in transactions, or scalable bot abuse. Perfect implementation cannot compensate for absent controls.

## Mitigation

Threat modeling in design phase; secure design patterns; abuse-case testing; segregate tenants; align with OWASP SAMM; security requirements in user stories; rate limiting and anti-automation for business logic.

## Replication Steps

1. Map critical workflows (payments, discounts, registration, password reset).
2. Test for logic abuse, race conditions, and state manipulation.
3. Review threat model and design docs for missing controls.
4. Attempt to violate stated business rules.
5. Document design-level gaps separately from coding defects.
