## Description

Buffer overflow on stack memory. Re-entered CWE Top 25:2025 at #14.

## Impact

RCE or crash in native code.

## Mitigation

Stack canaries; safe APIs; memory-safe languages; patching.

## Replication Steps

1. Identify native services.
2. Fuzz in lab.
3. Correlate crashes with CVEs.
4. Document version.
5. Recommend upgrade.

## Detection

**Host:** Stack guard violations; DEP/NX bypass attempts.

## Guidance

Group with CWE-120, CWE-787.
