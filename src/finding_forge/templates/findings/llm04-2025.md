## Description

Poisoning manipulates pre-training, fine-tuning, or embedding data to introduce backdoors, bias, or degraded safety behavior.

## Impact

Subverted model behavior at scale; targeted misclassification or hidden triggers affecting specific inputs.

## Mitigation

Validate training data provenance; anomaly detection on datasets; secure fine-tuning pipelines; monitor model behavior drift; access controls on data stores.

## Replication Steps

1. Review data ingestion and labeling pipelines.
2. Test for anomalous behavior on trigger-like inputs (if suspected).
3. Assess who can modify training or embedding corpora.
4. Verify integrity checks on datasets.
5. Document poisoning risk exposure.
