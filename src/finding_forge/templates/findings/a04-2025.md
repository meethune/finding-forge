## Description

Cryptographic failures involve missing or weak encryption, poor key management, weak randomness, deprecated algorithms, or cleartext transmission/storage of sensitive data. Previously categorized as 'Sensitive Data Exposure' in earlier Top 10 editions.

## Impact

Sensitive data (credentials, PII, payment data, health records) may be intercepted, decrypted offline, or exposed at rest. Regulatory penalties (GDPR, PCI DSS) often apply.

## Mitigation

Classify data; encrypt in transit (TLS 1.2+); encrypt sensitive data at rest; use strong password hashing (Argon2, scrypt, PBKDF2); proper key management and rotation; disable weak ciphers; use authenticated encryption; plan for post-quantum cryptography.

## Replication Steps

1. Map data flows for sensitive fields.
2. Test TLS configuration and certificate validation.
3. Identify cleartext protocols and storage.
4. Review password hashing and key material handling.
5. Document weak algorithms (MD5, SHA1, ECB, etc.).
