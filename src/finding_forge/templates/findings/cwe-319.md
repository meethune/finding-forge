## Description

Sensitive data sent without encryption (HTTP, SMTP, FTP). Mapped under A04:2025.

## Impact

Passive interception of credentials and session tokens on networks.

## Mitigation

Enforce HTTPS/HSTS; disable cleartext protocols; secure email and file transfer.

## Replication Steps

1. Identify HTTP and legacy protocol usage.
2. Capture traffic in lab to confirm cleartext secrets.
3. Test HSTS and redirect behavior.
4. Document affected flows.
5. Recommend TLS enforcement.
