## Description

Use of MD5, SHA1, DES, RC4, or weak TLS configurations. Mapped under A04:2025.

## Impact

Confidentiality loss via offline attacks; compliance failures.

## Mitigation

Modern algorithms (AES-GCM, SHA-256+); TLS 1.2+; disable weak ciphers; crypto agility plan.

## Replication Steps

1. Scan TLS with sslyze/testssl or similar.
2. Review application crypto usage.
3. Identify deprecated algorithms.
4. Document affected services.
5. Recommend configuration baselines.

## Detection

**Network:** Weak cipher negotiation; SSLv3/TLS1.0 handshakes.
